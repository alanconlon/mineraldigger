South of England Mineral Show on 17th and 18th October 2026 at South of England Event Centre, Ardingly, Sussex, RH17 6TL

Over the 17th and 18th October 2026 we are hosting the South of England Mineral Show, in the Queen's Jubilee Hall (use the North Gate and then the Green Gate) at the South of England Showground, RH17 6TL.

It will be a fantastic weekend of beautiful crystals, fascinating fossils, semi-precious beads, rare minerals and dazzling jewellery from a great selection of sellers.

From 10am to 4pm both Saturday and Sunday you'll have two days packed full of opportunity.

Adult entries are £5 on the door or you can purchase tickets on here. Children under 5 go free.

Free parking for all attendees.
